Fix-it v0, a concept for Ganit

Concept prototype for a product case study. Not a Bhanzu product. Everything runs in your browser and nothing is sent anywhere.

What this is

A working sketch of what my case study proposes for Ganit, so you can try it instead of reading about it.

1. The moment. A wrong answer in a lesson check, played both ways: the answer straight away, or one question first. This is the A/B test in Section 1.

2. My mistakes. The first seven are the real ones Ganit's "Check my work" found in my test pages on 26 Nov 2026. Today the app finds them, and nothing brings them back. Here they are kept.

3. Fix-it five. Five questions a day: look-alikes of the child's own mistakes with new numbers, two from the chapter the class is on, and a check that older fixes have held. Each wrong option is tagged with the slip that produces it, so the question that follows is instant.

4. Sunday report. The one message a parent gets. It rewrites itself as mistakes are fixed.

Two of the seven mistakes start as fixed, and the report starts with example numbers, so the screens are not empty. Aarav is a made-up child.
